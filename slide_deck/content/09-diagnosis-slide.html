When something goes wrong, ask first: is this a training problem or a prompt problem?

Training problem

The model does not know something it was never trained on, or exhibits a systematic behavior baked in during post-training. No amount of prompt tweaking will fix it. You either switch models or restructure the task.

Signal: the problem is consistent and reproducible regardless of how you phrase the prompt.

Prompt problem

The model is missing context you could provide — your data format, your conventions, your constraints. You have real levers to pull: rewrite the instruction, add examples, load relevant files, define what "done" means.

Signal: the problem disappears when you give the model the missing context.

Most problems researchers encounter are prompt problems. That is good news because it means the researcher has control.
